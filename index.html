Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The app collects no data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts and no server of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Stored on the device. Everything the user enters or adds — documents, entries, photos and settings — stays in the app's own storage on the device. It is deleted with the app and is included in the device's backups if those are switched on. The sign-in is checked on the device; nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Between two devices. The app can keep two devices of the same crew in agreement over a direct, encrypted Bluetooth link. No data passes through any server.

Public information on request. When the user asks for it, the app downloads public weather information from public services. A request names only the place it is for; it contains nothing about the user.

The app is not directed at children. If it ever starts doing anything else with data, this page will say so before that version is released.
