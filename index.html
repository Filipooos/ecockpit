Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Your account. The app opens for a personal account issued to the crew. The account holds your e-mail address, your name, a password (stored only in hashed form) and whether the account is approved. It is kept by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and used only to decide who may open the app. You can delete it at any time in the app (Settings › Account › Delete account) or by writing to us.

How the app is used. While you are signed in, the app records which of its screens you open and for how long, and which of its steps you complete — for example importing a flight, signing a record or printing the journey log — each with the time, the app version and the type of device. It sends this with your account so that the people running the app can see how it is used. Nothing shown on a screen or typed into the app is recorded, and nothing about the flight. This record is deleted with the account.

Feedback. If the people running the app have switched it on for your account, you can send them a note from the app (Settings › Send feedback), with a screenshot if you choose to attach one. The note, the screenshot, the screen you came from, the app version and the type of device are kept with your account so that they can read and answer it. They are deleted with the account.

Flights. Flights you add yourself — documents, entries, photos and settings — stay in the app's own storage on the device, in a part that belongs to your account; another account on the same device does not see them. They are never sent to us. Flights may also be provided to your account; the app then downloads them.

Between two devices. The app can keep two devices of the same crew in agreement over a direct, encrypted Bluetooth link. No flight data passes through any server.

Public information on request. When you ask for it, the app downloads public weather information from public services. A request names only the place it is for; it contains nothing about you.

Nothing else. No advertising, no tracking, no data sold or shared with anyone for their own purposes. The app is not directed at children. If it starts doing anything else with data, this page will say so before that version is released.
