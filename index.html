Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy describes what the app does with data. It is short because the app does very little with it.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no server of its own. Nothing about the flight or the crew is sent to the developer or to any third party.

The app asks for a user name and a password once after it is installed. Both are checked on the device itself; the user name is kept on the device and is never sent anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app holds is stored on the device itself, inside the app’s own storage area:

  • the briefing packs imported by the crew, and everything read out of them
  • the entries the crew make: times, fuel figures, clearances, notes, the journey log, and handwritten signatures
  • the app’s own settings, such as which seat the pilot occupies and which sector is open

This data is removed when the app is deleted, and is included in the device’s own backups if the user has those switched on.

What is transmitted

Between the two devices of one crew. The entries for the flight being operated travel over a direct Bluetooth Low Energy link, to keep both pilots’ copies of the same paperwork in agreement.

  • It reaches only a device holding the same flight plan; the pairing key is derived from the plan itself.
  • The messages are encrypted (ChaChaPoly, with the key derived using HKDF).
  • No data passes through any server.

Weather, when the crew asks for it. When the crew taps a download button, or writes the off-block time, the app downloads weather for the route from public meteorological services: METAR and TAF reports (aviationweather.gov), SIGMETs, radar, satellite images, model data and charts (NOAA, NASA, EUMETNET, Environment and Climate Change Canada, Deutscher Wetterdienst, ČHMÚ, LFV). Each request names only the aerodromes or the geographic area whose weather is wanted. Like any internet request, it is seen by the service that answers it together with the device’s network address; nothing about the flight, the crew or the device’s owner is included. The weather is kept on the device.

Sharing a journey log

The crew can print the journey log or export it as a PDF. Where that document then goes is entirely the crew’s own action — the app only hands it to the system share sheet or to a printer.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something else with data, this page will say so before that version is released.
